Refund Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase from MITEX. Because we sell digital products, our refund policy is designed to be fair to both buyers and our team.

1. Eligible for Full Refund

You qualify for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your purchase, AND
  • You have not accessed the website download/delivery link.

2. Not Eligible for Refund

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • You have already accessed or downloaded the website via the delivery link.
  • The refund request is made more than 7 days after the purchase date.
  • The website was customized by MITEX staff according to your specifications and the work has already begun.
  • You changed your mind after downloading or using the website.
  • Issues caused by your own modifications to the website code after purchase.
  • Third-party hosting or domain-related issues (not related to the website itself).

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, send an email to admin@mitex.store with:

  • Your full name
  • The email address used for the purchase
  • Your order reference number
  • The reason for your refund request

We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and provide a decision within 5 business days.

4. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Paystack.
  • Processing time: 5–10 business days after approval, depending on your bank.
  • You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed.

5. Partial Refunds

In rare cases where custom work has been partially completed, a partial refund may be offered at our discretion, minus the cost of work already performed.

6. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may:

  • Reply to our email with additional information for review.
  • Request escalation to a senior team member.
  • Initiate a payment dispute through Paystack or your bank (note: chargebacks will be defended with transaction evidence).

7. Contact

For refund requests or questions about this policy:

  • Email: admin@mitex.store
  • WhatsApp: +234 701 163 3770
