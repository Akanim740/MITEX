Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

MITEX ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at mitex.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, password (encrypted), phone number, date of birth, and country when you create an account.
  • Payment information: When you make a purchase, payment is processed by Paystack. We do not store your card number, CVV, or bank details on our servers. If you opt in to one-tap checkout, Paystack gives us a secure token that lets us charge the card on future purchases — we never see or store the actual card number.
  • Identity verification (workers): Staff and workers must submit an 11-digit NIN or BVN. These are encrypted at rest and only visible to MITEX administrators.
  • Usage data: Browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on our platform, collected through cookies and server logs.
  • Communication data: Messages you send us through contact forms, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Employment applications: If you apply to work with us, we collect your name, email, phone, and any work samples you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process purchases and deliver the websites you buy.
  • To send transactional emails (order confirmations, delivery notifications, password resets).
  • To communicate with you about enquiries, support requests, or employment applications.
  • To improve our platform, products, and services.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Paystack (paystack.com). When you make a purchase, Paystack collects your card or bank details directly. We only receive your email, name, and a payment reference — we never see or store your full card number. If you enable one-tap checkout, Paystack stores a token on our behalf; you can remove it at any time from your account settings, after which one-tap charges stop.

4. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Session cookie (mitex_refresh): An httpOnly cookie used to keep you logged in. It contains a refresh token that allows us to issue new access tokens without requiring you to sign in again.
  • Local storage: We store your access token, user preferences (country, language), and theme settings in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with the following third-party services necessary to operate MITEX:

  • Paystack — Payment processing.
  • Render — Website hosting and infrastructure.
  • Supabase — Database hosting.
  • SMTP provider — Transactional email delivery.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (e.g., financial transaction records for tax purposes).

7. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@mitex.store.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, JWT-based authentication, encrypted password storage (bcrypt), and role-based access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

MITEX is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a prominent notice on our website. Your continued use of MITEX after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
