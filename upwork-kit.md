## 3. Bidding Strategy (the part that actually matters)

### Do's
- **Apply in the first 1-3 hours** of a job posting. Early proposals rank higher.
- **Always open with** the client's name and reference their project. Never paste a generic proposal.
- **Write 2-4 short paragraphs.** Clients scan. Long walls of text get skipped.
- **Add a question at the end** ("Could you share X?") — it forces a reply and builds a conversation.
- **Be specific about deliverable time.** "Ready in 6 days" beats "fast delivery."
- **Send 5-10 proposals per day**, consistently. It's a numbers game.

### Avoid
- ❌ Copy-pasting the same proposal to every job (Upwork filters these out)
- ❌ Bidding rock-bottom ($5 jobs). They attract nightmare clients and 1-star reviews.
- ❌ Taking jobs outside your skill level to "build confidence" — protect your JSS (Job Success Score).
- ❌ Applying to jobs with zero description. If the client didn't write anything, they usually ghost.
